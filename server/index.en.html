Guardián Web

The browser extension that warns you about dangerous sites without spying on you — and, with every warning, teaches you to recognize the danger.

Install on Chrome View the code
No telemetry · Open source · Local mode

Security that respects your privacy

Most "free" security extensions protect you in exchange for your browsing history. Guardián Web does the opposite: it runs on your own device, never sends your browsing to any server, and its code is open and auditable. Turn on 100% local mode and nothing leaves your machine.

What it detects

🎣

Phishing & impersonation

Domains that mimic brands and tricks like “g00gle” or characters that fool the eye (homographs).

💳

Card theft

Forms that send your card number or data to another domain (skimmers).

⛏️

Cryptojacking

Pages that mine cryptocurrency using your processor without permission.

🔒

Scams & hijacks

The classic “your computer is infected, call now” and pages that lock your browser.

🕵️

Tracking & fingerprinting

Covert identification and domains that follow you across the web.

⬇️

Dangerous downloads

Executables disguised as PDFs and files with double extensions.

Unlike other tools, it doesn't just match the URL against a list: it analyzes behavior, so it catches brand-new threats not yet on any list.

🇪🇸 Built for Spain, too

It specifically recognizes impersonation attempts against the brands and institutions most faked in SMS and email campaigns:

  • BBVA, CaixaBank, Santander
  • Bizum
  • Tax Agency (AEAT)
  • Correos (postal service)
  • DGT (traffic authority)
  • Social Security · SEPE
  • Cl@ve
  • Telecom & energy companies

📚 And it teaches, too

Every warning includes a link to learn about that threat (OWASP, EFF, MDN). The goal isn't only to protect you, but to help you recognize danger yourself. Because the best defense is you, knowing how to look.

Privacy, specifically

  • 🚫 No telemetry or analytics. Your activity is never collected.
  • 🙈 No account, no sign-up. We don't ask for email or personal data.
  • 🏠 Optional local mode. Disables every external query.
  • 🔐 What you type stays with you. Compared on your device; never stored or sent.
  • 🤝 Community reputation with k-anonymity. On lookups, the server never knows which site you're visiting.
  • 🔎 Open source. Anyone can review exactly what it does.
